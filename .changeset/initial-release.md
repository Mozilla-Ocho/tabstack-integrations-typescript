Initial release: Tabstack tools for LangChain.js and the Vercel AI SDK, wrapping the official
`@tabstack/sdk`. Both adapters expose `extract_structured_data`, `extract_page_content`,
`research_question`, `generate_structured_data`, and `automate_browser_task`, sharing one
framework-agnostic core.
